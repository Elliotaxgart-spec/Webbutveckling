About Mercedes

Mercedes-Benz is one of the oldest and most famous car brands in the world. Its history started in 1886 when Karl Benz created the Benz Patent-Motorwagen, which is considered the first gasoline-powered car. Later, the companies of Karl Benz and Gottlieb Daimler merged in 1926 to form Mercedes-Benz. The name “Mercedes” came from Mercédès Jellinek, whose father was an early car dealer and racing enthusiast. Over the years, Mercedes-Benz became known for luxury, innovation, and performance, producing many famous cars and technologies that influenced the modern automobile industry. Today, the company continues to build luxury vehicles, SUVs, and electric cars under its electric division Mercedes-EQ.
