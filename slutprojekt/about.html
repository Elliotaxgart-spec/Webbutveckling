About BMW

BMW (Bayerische Motoren Werke AG) is a German luxury automobile manufacturer founded in 1916. Originally producing aircraft engines, BMW expanded into motorcycles in 1923 and automobiles in 1928. Known for its performance, engineering precision, and iconic design, BMW has become one of the most recognized luxury car brands in the world, producing everything from sporty coupes to electric vehicles. Today, BMW continues to innovate while maintaining its reputation for driving pleasure and quality.
