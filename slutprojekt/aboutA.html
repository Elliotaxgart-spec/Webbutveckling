Audi was founded in 1909 by the engineer August Horch in Zwickau. The name Audi comes from Latin and means “listen.” It is the Latin translation of Horch, which means “listen!” in German. In the 1930s, Audi joined with three other car companies to form Auto Union. This is where Audi’s four-ring logo comes from, representing the four companies that merged. After World War II, the company was rebuilt and later became part of the Volkswagen Group in the 1960s. Today, Audi is known for luxury cars, sporty models, and advanced technology, including quattro all-wheel drive and cars like the Audi R8.
